For a flagged/stretch new-grad profile, **referrals beat cold applications**. Tracked in `Resumes/Applications/Referrals.md`.

Priority asks: Razorpay → IDfy → Perfios → Cashfree → Setu.
- **Manav Aggarwal** — Aaryan's manager → channel into **Glean** (knows Nityang, the Glean recruiter who'd contacted Manav). Drafted a cold-but-referral-routed message to Nityang, tone-matched, with a **≤200-char** connection-note version (the real LinkedIn limit).
- **Pranav Gangrade** — 1st-degree connection **at Navi** → referral route for the APM application.

### How to take a referral (the playbook)
Ask for an **intro**, not a resume-drop (intros convert better and let you tailor). Give the referrer a **short, specific company list** so they just connect you. Always offer the tailored `.pdf`. Send a thank-you and close the loop on outcomes.
