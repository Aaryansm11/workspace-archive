## First Time Right (FTR) — the theme
- **Stabilization:** 2 weeks of **<0.5% issues & <3 retries** within 30 days of go-live — top 90% of accounts,
  **100% of top BFSI**.
- **BI delivery:** within **90 days**; 100% of accounts get a BI metrics doc.
- **Integration CSAT:** avg **8/10**, with **≥40% response** rate.
- **POC success rate** above target (for zero-one geography SAs, post adoption by 2 of top-100 clients).

## Output / leaderboard metrics
- BIs signed off by lead · BRDs+solution docs signed off · POC success criteria identified ·
  product features/gaps raised ([[JIRA]]) · opportunities identified.
