## Reading it
- **Best pay + fit:** Postman, Razorpay, Setu, for Solutions Engineer or backend SDE (~₹20L to ₹27L).
- **Best domain fit:** IDfy (identity/KYC), even though pay is lower.
- Most of these **beat Yellow.ai (~₹10L)** and match or beat **Navi APM (~₹15L)**, so the referral route is also the better-paying route.
